Ultimate Baby Tracker

Runs on your own machine

One tap at 3am.
Answers at the check-up.

Feeds, nappies, sleep, medicine and anything else you invent — logged with one thumb by whoever is holding the baby, and added up into the numbers a pediatrician actually asks for.

  • TrackOne tap per event. Feeds and sleep run as timers. Undo for a few seconds after every tap.
  • HistoryEvery entry, and who logged it. Grouped by day, filterable, editable, exports to CSV.
  • Stats1 to 90 days. Intake per kilo, sleep, nappies and nutrients. Each chart saves as an image.
  • Alarms“Tell me if she hasn’t fed in three hours.” Rings on time, even in a background tab.
